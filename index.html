Privacy Policy for Puzzle Pix

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Puzzle Pix is developed by JNitro Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzle Pix handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Puzzle Pix does not collect personal information from users. The application does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

Photos and Images

Puzzle Pix allows users to select images from their Android device to create puzzles.

Selected images are processed locally on the user's device. Puzzle Pix does not upload user images to a developer-operated server and does not transmit them to third parties.

Puzzle images and related files may be stored locally on the user's device as part of the application's functionality.

Internet Access

Puzzle Pix does not require an internet connection for its core functionality and does not transmit user images or personal information to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and Analytics

Puzzle Pix does not contain advertisements and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Puzzle Pix does not maintain personal user information on developer-controlled servers.

Images and puzzle-related files stored on the user's device remain under the user's control and may be removed using the device's normal file-management or app-management functions, where applicable.

Children's Privacy

Puzzle Pix does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because the application does not require personal information to operate.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
